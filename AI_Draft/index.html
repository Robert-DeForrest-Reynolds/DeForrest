    5. Datumatics: the theory

    Entitled 'Datumatics' to codify a colloquial name, but can be called whatever you may like, as long as we have something commonly to refer to. DFWM (DeForrest Workflow Methodology) is codified directly as Datumatics: "There's really no reason to separate the ideas." Codified

    5.1 Definitions, in order of appearance

    Source Definition
    Macro.md (Datumatics glossary copy) Historical "A theory of computation where all systems are modeled as a protocol over structured state spaces defined by addressing, projection, transformation, composition, and evaluation operators." A "protocol of knowledge (datum) interaction."
    Baseline, "coined" paragraph Codified "A theory that claims observational structure, and computational-structure are derivable, and composable models, that as a protocol, can be applied over structured states and spaces defined by addressing, projection, transformation, composition, and evaluation operators." I.e. a way for humans to store, retrieve, and transfer knowledge between physical and metaphysical realities.
    Baseline glossary Codified "A theory of computational-structure where all observations can be classified as systems of structure that can modeled as a protocol over observable and structured state using addressing, projection, transformation, composition, and processing."
    Baseline glossary, extended Codified "I.e. a peer-to-peer knowledge-interaction protocol that facilitates empirical recordation of human knowledge, unrestricted to external existences; an atomic and dependency-free knowledge management protocol for peer-to-peer transaction."
    Baseline tagline Codified "A way to organize, and refine digital literacy across generations." "A methodology for organizing, storing, and transferring a personal knowledgebase across generations, built on local-first ownership and a single underlying data theory."

    Computer Science & Datumatics: an encompassing phrase for the study of computation, physics, and information within a single frame of reference, grounded in morphology. [Earlier: "Computer Science & Informatics".]

    Purpose of codification: reclassify and re-evaluate the study of computation, electronics, information systems, and decision-making. Posit: all computational systems reduce to a small set of primitive interaction operations over structured state representations.

    There are three fundamental "subjects of reference framing" (Storing is the one detailed; see §6). "I'll keep it simple for now, and expose complexity as we go."

    5.2 The five operators

    Operator Codified meaning (examples) Earlier meaning (Macro.md)
    Addressing Spatialization of datum (search, query, pointer resolution) Selecting a region of datum space
    Projection Morphology, interpretation (parsing tables, query results, AST subtrees, slices) Extracting a view (query results, AST subtrees, slices)
    Transformation Rewriting or updating abstractives (compile, refactor, update index) Rewriting or updating structure
    Composition Combining abstractives (joins, function composition, merges) Combining structures (joins, function composition, graph merges)
    Processing Function over time; collapse into value or further abstractive behavior (execution, inference, ranking) Evaluation: collapsing structure into value or behavior

    5.3 Glossary Codified

    Term Definition
    Observation A tangible element of reality.
    Implementation Human-based interaction with or upon an observation; usually the interaction itself then becomes a datum.
    Datum An encapsulated element of observation or implementation.
    Data An encapsulated and congruent set of datums.
    Abstractive A classified, and recomposable datum. (Absent from the older Macro.md glossary.)
    Relation A traversable connection between multiple datums.
    Record A datum that has been documented.
    Metadata External data linked to a datum or data.
    Digital Station "Points of access" within a computer for real-time work (working with computers, not on them); macro-access points over a greater system of smaller implements and sub-domains.
    Digital Module A collection of interoperational digital stations and/or microtools to facilitate a workflow.
    Microtool A standalone executable that is singular in implementation detail.

    Primitives. There are "explicit primitives" and "implicit primitives". Explicit primitives are treated as absolutely defined, usable, and recomposable. Implicit primitives are undefined and usable as a metaphysical ideal.

    Frame equations. Frame & Point == Datum. Context Window == Point & Data ("Structure of Data"). Context Window & Data == Frame of Reference. (The DFWM list also named Datum, Data, Point, Frame, Frame of Reference as terms.)

    5.4 Statements Codified

    • A datum at rest carries valence (the ability to be observed happening); observation actualizes valence; the unit presently in observation is the abstractive.
    • Datum: unit at rest, addressable.
    • Abstractive: unit in observation, operand.
    • Knowledgebase (ULL): unit in transit; documented form projectable by another mind.
    • Codified output types are required; Any is not viable.
    • A datum with perfect and apparent valence is preferred to a datum with chaotic valence.

    5.5 Knowledge primitives, composites and processes Codified

    Primitives

    • Datum == Unit of Knowledge == Abstraction == Value — the fundamental knowledge unit.
    • Abstractive == Contextually Present Unit of Knowledge == Instantiate — a datum being observed.
    • Language == Artificial Symbolism for Mnemonic & Empirical Communication — a methodology for empirically driven communication.
    • Compositor == Constructor == Language Unit == Mapping — units of language as abstractives.

    Composites

    • Set == Data == Relational == Extensional — a collection, compilation, or linking of units.
    • Process == Function == Methodology — a description of a thing happening over time.
    • Motion == Time == Transform — an observation of a description of a thing happening over time.
    • Ordered == Contiguous — things permutated in a single observation, without interstice.
    • Valence == Weight == Mass == Energy — a thing's ability to be observed happening.
    • Isomorphic == Identical == Univalent — the trait of a datum being observably identical to another datum.

    Processes

    • Searching == Filtering == Indexing
    • Compilation == Rendering == Composition
    • Process == Transforming == (left incomplete in source)

    5.6 Q&A Codified

    Q: Why "datums" instead of data? A: To connect better in idealization with working individual units of data: datums. A function of first-order logic, or first-principle thinking in how data is structured, and its processes before it's worked on by said processes.

    Q: What is an "abstractive unit" versus a "datum unit"? A: An abstractive unit is a datum within a working context, or within observance. A datum unit is held in storage "at a resting state". Both are units of knowledge.

    5.7 Truth, certainty and knowns Codified

    Authority of Truth & Social Contracts of Proofs of Truth

    • Treat local storage as the primary source of truth; distributed systems are backups, not authority.
    • You, a single individual, are the authority of the primary source of truth. A distributed primary source of truth only works as a standard agreement, never as a certifiable contract.
    • Communal distributions of proofs of truth are the only valid social contract with empirical value; not the proposed truths themselves. Only an individual within their own mind can determine truth with certainty. The value of a social contract lies in the empirical value of the proof of truth.

    The Table of Certainty

    100% 99% 50% 0% 50% 99% 100%
    False Falsey Falsish Unknown Truthish Truthy True

    The four types of knowns: Known Known · Unknown Known · Known Unknown · Unknown Unknown.

    "This is not meant to find unknown knowledge, it's meant to record known knowledge."

    5.8 Peer-to-peer transfer model Codified

    • Localized compilers on receiving peer-to-peer machines parse/decode the given information. All processes are local, defined by the given parse.
    • Parses are always manually executed, otherwise automated only by the receiving user's own implementation.
    • What is transferred is text-only: how things are to be parsed and processed. Only the receiving, localized, virtual compiler parses and processes the content, then presents it according to the user's defined interface.
    • The Anonymous Enhancement Proposal. A peer-to-peer proposal method for information review and refinement. Anonymous by default. Lets a peer create a review of someone else's current abstractives. "Abstractive Argumentation": a base methodology for peer-to-peer grounding of truth.
    • Diff-based Peer-to-Peer Sync. A sync that depicts the difference between two knowledgebases.

    5.9 Formats to represent with Datumatics Codified

    The Atom · Periodic Table · A Person · Dictionary · Graphic · E=MC (sic) · Math Formula · Image · Video · Event · Relationship Tree · Document · Website.

    5.10 Inspirations of the methodology Codified

    Datumatics is foundationally inspired by the era of UNIX, and embraces the market requirements Apple exposed: computation as a utility outside the hands of technicalists. UNIX captured computing and its utilitarian use between 1965 and 1978; Apple redefined colloquial understanding of what computing was and how it was used.

    The document aims to be digestible in one or two sittings: a "foundational reference manual for organization based for high-stress, and high-skill use"; a "standardized grounding for digital literacy between platforms and environments" giving someone "a ground to stand on". Customization and extension are encouraged. Following the base at minimum yields a modern, secure, portable knowledgebase and knowledge-management system.
