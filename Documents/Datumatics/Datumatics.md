## 5.2 The five operators

| Operator | Codified meaning (examples) | Earlier meaning (Macro.md) |

| **Addressing** | Spatialization of datum (search, query, pointer resolution) | Selecting a region of datum space |

## 5.6 Q&amp;A *[Codified]*

&gt; **Q:** Why "datums" instead of data?
&gt; **A:** To connect better in idealization with working individual units of data: datums. A function of first-order logic, or first-principle thinking in *how data is structured, and its processes* **before** *it's worked on by said processes*.

&gt; **Q:** What is an "abstractive unit" versus a "datum unit"?
&gt; **A:** An abstractive unit is a datum within a working context, or within observance. A datum unit is held in storage "at a resting state". Both are *units of knowledge*.

## 5.7 Truth, certainty and knowns *[Codified]*

**Authority of Truth &amp; Social Contracts of Proofs of Truth**

- Treat local storage as the primary source of truth; distributed systems are backups, not authority.
- **You**, a **single individual**, are the authority of the primary source of truth. A distributed primary source of truth *only works as a standard agreement, never as a certifiable contract.*
- Communal distributions of *proofs* of truth are the only valid social contract with empirical value; not the proposed truths themselves. Only an individual within their own mind can determine truth with certainty. The value of a social contract lies in the empirical value of the proof of truth.

**The Table of Certainty**

| 100% | 99% | 50% | 0% | 50% | 99% | 100% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| False | Falsey | Falsish | Unknown | Truthish | Truthy | True |

**The four types of knowns:** Known Known · Unknown Known · Known Unknown · Unknown Unknown.

"This is not meant to **find** unknown knowledge, it's meant to **record** known knowledge."

## 5.8 Peer-to-peer transfer model *[Codified]*

- Localized compilers on receiving peer-to-peer machines parse/decode the given information. All processes are local, defined by the given parse.
- Parses are *always* manually executed, otherwise automated only by the receiving user's own implementation.
- What is transferred is text-only: *how* things are to be parsed and processed. Only the receiving, localized, virtual compiler parses and processes the content, then presents it according to the user's defined interface.
- **The Anonymous Enhancement Proposal.** A peer-to-peer proposal method for information review and refinement. Anonymous by default. Lets a peer create a review of someone else's current abstractives. "Abstractive Argumentation": a base methodology for peer-to-peer grounding of truth.
- **Diff-based Peer-to-Peer Sync.** A sync that depicts the difference between two knowledgebases.

## 5.10 Inspirations of the methodology *[Codified]*

Datumatics is foundationally inspired by the era of UNIX, and embraces the market requirements Apple exposed: computation as a utility outside the hands of technicalists. UNIX captured computing and its utilitarian use between 1965 and 1978; Apple redefined colloquial understanding of *what* computing was and how it was used.

The document aims to be digestible in one or two sittings: a "foundational reference manual for organization based for high-stress, and high-skill use"; a "standardized grounding for digital literacy between platforms and environments" giving someone "a ground to stand on". Customization and extension are encouraged. Following the base at minimum yields a modern, secure, portable knowledgebase and knowledge-management system.
